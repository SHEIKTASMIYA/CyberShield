Frequently asked questions

How quickly should I report a fraud? +
Report immediately. Funds can move across multiple accounts within minutes — the faster a freeze request is raised, the higher the chance of recovery.
What evidence should I upload? +
Screenshots of the transaction, any call recordings, SMS/email from the suspect, and your bank statement covering the transaction date.
How long does fund recovery take? +
Once funds are frozen, recovery typically takes 7–15 business days depending on adjudication and any legal proceedings.
Can I edit my complaint after submitting? +
You can't edit core transaction details, but you can add evidence and notes any time from your case page.
