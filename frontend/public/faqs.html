Frequently asked questions

Answers for citizens and institutions using CyberShield.

Is CyberShield a bank or payment processor? +
No. CyberShield is a coordination platform only. It never holds, moves, or processes funds — all money movement stays within each bank's own systems.
How fast should I report a fraud? +
Immediately. Funds can move across several accounts within minutes of a fraud. The sooner a freeze request is raised, the higher the chance of recovery.
Can banks see all of each other's data? +
No. Institutions only see the case-relevant information needed to act — source banks don't see a destination bank's full account details, and vice versa.
How does telecom verification help? +
SIM and IMEI checks help investigators confirm whether a phone number or device used in a fraud matches its registered owner, or has been used across multiple cases.
How long does fund recovery take? +
Once funds are frozen, recovery typically takes 7–15 business days, depending on adjudication and any legal proceedings required.
How do institutions get connected to CyberShield? +
Banks and telecom operators are onboarded by the CyberShield program office. Reach out via the Contact page to begin institutional onboarding.
