Acceptance of terms

By accessing CyberShield, you agree to use the platform only for legitimate fraud reporting, investigation, and coordination purposes.

Accurate reporting

Citizens filing complaints must provide accurate, truthful information. Filing a knowingly false report is a punishable offense under applicable IT Act provisions.

Institutional use

Banks, telecom operators, and government users must access only cases within their jurisdiction and authorization, and must act within the SLA commitments established by the platform.

No banking functionality

CyberShield does not process payments, hold funds, or replace any institution's core banking or telecom infrastructure. All financial actions occur within each institution's own systems.

Limitation of liability

CyberShield facilitates coordination but does not guarantee fund recovery, which depends on timely institutional action and legal/adjudication outcomes outside the platform's control.

Changes to these terms

These terms may be updated periodically. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.
