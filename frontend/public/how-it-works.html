How it Works

From report to recovery, one coordinated timeline

Five stages connect a victim's complaint to a resolved case — with every institution acting on the same information.

01

Report

A victim files a complaint through the Citizen Portal with transaction details, a description of what happened, and supporting evidence like screenshots or call recordings.

02

Triage

The case is automatically scored by amount, channel, and pattern, then routed to a Cyber Crime officer with matching jurisdiction and capacity.

03

Verify

The source bank confirms the transaction against its own records and verifies the account holder's identity before any freeze request is raised.

04

Freeze

The destination bank reviews the freeze request against a visible SLA clock and places a hold on the beneficiary account where warranted.

05

Recover

Once the Cyber Crime Department completes its investigation, frozen funds are released back to the victim through standard banking and legal processes.

CyberShield

A coordination platform for the Cyber Crime Department, connecting banks and telecom operators to victims.
