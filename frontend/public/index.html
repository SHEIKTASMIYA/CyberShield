Built for coordination

One case, four institutions, one timeline

CyberShield doesn't process payments or hold funds. It gives every party in a fraud case the same evidence, the same clock, and the same next step.

Real-time case routing

Complaints are triaged by amount, channel, and pattern the moment they're filed, and routed to the right officer automatically.

Cross-bank freeze requests

Source banks raise freeze requests directly to destination banks, with a visible SLA clock both sides can track.

Fraud network graph

Investigators see linked accounts, UPI IDs, devices, and phone numbers across cases — surfacing repeat-offender patterns.

Evidentiary chain of custody

Every upload, note, and status change is timestamped and logged for court-admissible audit trails.

Telecom verification

SIM and IMEI checks are requested and returned inside the same case — no separate emails or phone calls.

Command-level analytics

District and state-level dashboards track recovery rate, SLA compliance, and institution performance.

How it works

From report to recovery

1
Report

Victim files a complaint with transaction details and evidence.

2
Triage

Case is scored and assigned to a Cyber Crime officer.

3
Verify

Source bank confirms the transaction and account details.

4
Freeze

Destination bank reviews and actions the freeze request.

5
Recover

Funds are held and returned through standard banking process.
