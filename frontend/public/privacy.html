Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 July 2026

What we collect

CyberShield collects information you provide when filing a complaint — including transaction details, contact information, and evidence you upload — as well as information institutions submit while acting on a case.

How we use it

Data is used solely to coordinate fraud response between you, your bank, the destination bank, telecom operators, and the Cyber Crime Department. It is not used for marketing, sold, or shared with unrelated third parties.

Who can see your data

Only institutions directly involved in your case can view case-relevant details. Access is role-based and every view is logged in an audit trail.

Data retention

Case data is retained in accordance with government record-keeping requirements for law-enforcement and evidentiary purposes, typically for the duration of any related legal proceedings plus a statutory retention period.

Your rights

You may request a copy of your case data or ask questions about how it's used by contacting our support team through the Contact page.
