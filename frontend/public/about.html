About CyberShield

A coordination layer, not a replacement

CyberShield was built to close the gap between the moment a fraud is reported and the moment the right institution can act on it.

Our mission

Cyber fraud moves fast — often across three or four institutions within minutes. Historically, victims, banks, telecom operators, and the Cyber Crime Department have coordinated over phone calls and emails, losing critical time. CyberShield exists to close that gap with a shared, real-time case timeline.

What we are

CyberShield is a coordination platform. It does not process payments, hold funds, or replace any institution's core banking or telecom systems. It gives every party in a fraud case visibility into the same facts, evidence, and next steps.

Who we serve

  • Citizens who need a clear, guided way to report fraud and track recovery
  • Source and destination banks that need to verify, freeze, and act quickly
  • Telecom operators asked to verify SIM and device details for investigations
  • Cyber Crime Department officers who need one view across every case

Governance

CyberShield operates as a Government Cyber Crime Department initiative, with periodic audits and oversight aligned with national cybersecurity guidance.
