Platform Features

Everything needed to coordinate a fraud response

From the first complaint to final fund recovery, CyberShield gives every institution the same real-time picture of a case.

Real-time case routing

Complaints are triaged by amount, channel, and pattern the moment they're filed, and routed automatically to the right officer.

Cross-bank freeze requests

Source banks raise freeze requests directly to destination banks with a visible SLA clock both sides track.

Fraud network graph

Investigators see linked accounts, UPI IDs, devices, and phone numbers across cases to surface repeat-offender patterns.

Evidentiary chain of custody

Every upload, note, and status change is timestamped and logged for court-admissible audit trails.

Telecom verification

SIM and IMEI checks are requested and returned inside the same case — no separate emails or calls.

Command-level analytics

District and state-level dashboards track recovery rate, SLA compliance, and institution performance.

Role-based institutional portal

Source Banks, Destination Banks, Telecom, Cyber Crime and Admin each get a purpose-built console.

Guided citizen reporting

A step-by-step flow helps victims file complete, accurate complaints with the right evidence attached.

Secure messaging

Victims and officers communicate directly inside the case, keeping every exchange on the record.

CyberShield

A coordination platform for the Cyber Crime Department, connecting banks and telecom operators to victims. Does not replace banking software.
